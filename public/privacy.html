English — Privacy Policy for Mehrli qo'llar

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mehrli qo'llar Android application (package name: uz.mexrliqollar.app) and web platform.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account & Profile Data: Full name, phone number, role (worker or employer), and optional email address.
  • Identity Verification (KYC): Optional ID document and selfie images submitted solely by workers to obtain a verified safety badge.
  • Location Data: Foreground GPS coordinates used to display nearby workers or service orders on the map, calculate distance, and attach coordinates to emergency SOS alerts. Exact worker coordinates are masked prior to order acceptance.
  • Orders, Messages & Safety Contacts: Service order details, ratings, chat messages between matched parties, and user-chosen trusted contacts for SOS alerts.

2. How Data Is Used and Shared

Data is used strictly to operate the service marketplace, connect verified workers with employers, render maps (Yandex Maps / OpenStreetMap), and deliver safety/SOS notifications. We display no third-party ads and never sell personal data.

3. Data Retention, Security & Account Deletion

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS, and credentials (passwords/PINs) are stored as cryptographic hashes. Users may request permanent deletion of their account and personal data at any time by emailing omonovkamolbek0@gmail.com or support@mexrliqollar.uz.
